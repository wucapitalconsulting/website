About us

An independent partner on the Asia–Europe corridor

Nan Capital & Consulting is a boutique advisory firm serving Asian clients who invest in Europe. We combine an Asia-native understanding of our clients' objectives with on-the-ground execution across European markets.

As an independent firm, we advise on one side of the table only — yours. No product pushing, no conflicts of interest: discreet, senior-level counsel from opportunity sourcing through due diligence to closing and beyond.

"Cross-border investment succeeds when both sides of the bridge are understood — the capital and the market."

Our conviction

We work where our clients invest — with presence and partners in Asia and across Europe's key financial and industrial centres.
